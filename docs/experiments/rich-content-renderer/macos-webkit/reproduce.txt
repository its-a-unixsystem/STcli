Observed isolation:
- Effective entitlements are exactly app-sandbox plus network.client.
- Controller positive controls read the owned host sentinel and reached the owned loopback listener.
- sandbox_check denied helper and attributed WebKit service reads/writes of the sentinel.
- Hostile marker remained original, nested marker count was zero, and the listener received zero document requests.
- Digest-bound asset bytes supplied by the controller and served through `stcli-probe://` loaded at naturalWidth=128.
- The unchanged card's direct HTTPS emblem did not load. Fixtures were not rewritten; renderer documents intentionally have no direct web fetch.
- Two sequential documents returned empty cookies and created no persistent cookie/cache marker.
- App Sandbox `network.client` remains process-level outbound authority; document filtering does not remove it. ADR 0010's broker is for Extension `fetch` and secondary inference, while renderer assets are verified/content-addressed bytes supplied by the trusted controller over the local scheme.

Observed bounds and resources:
- Oversized HTML, single asset, aggregate assets, and output height returned their bounded errors.
- SIGTERM ended the helper in 0.00162 s, under 750 ms.
- Endpoint aggregate physical footprint was 418,108,048 bytes and ps RSS was 490,569,728 bytes. Both sums may double-count shared mappings. This footprint is accepted for the macOS helper experiment.
- Aggregate attributed threads were 64, below 256.
- Cold full paths were 0.654, 0.359, and 0.311 s. Warm medians/maxima are in macos-webkit-measurements.json.
- No cgroup-equivalent memory, CPU, or task enforcement was available; resource use is measured rather than enforced for this CLI experiment.

Cleanup:
After q, pgrep found no RichContentWebKitProbe or current rich_content_probe process. The helper container was removed by the evaluator. OS-managed shared WebKit services were not killed. Dedicated terminal evidence screenshots used Screen Recording permission; WKWebView takeSnapshot itself ran offscreen without Screen Recording permission or a visible app window.

Distribution reality:
- The terminal directly execs the signed app-bundle Mach-O without an IDE. The experiment uses an ad-hoc hardened-runtime signature, and direct terminal launch is the accepted model. Gatekeeper/Developer ID/notarization are relevant only to normal end-user distribution, which is outside this experiment. WebKit is OS-provided and OS-updated. AppKit requires a logged-in GUI/WindowServer session, but no foreground or visible helper window was required. LSMinimumSystemVersion declares 13.0; only macOS 26.4.1 arm64 was exercised.

Verdict:
- Functionally viable experiment under the clarified decisions. The footprint and direct CLI launch of the ad-hoc-signed bundle Mach-O are accepted. Renderer documents deliberately deny direct web fetches. ADR 0010 Brokered HTTPS Egress is reserved for Extension `fetch` and secondary-inference/provider calls; the renderer instead ingests trusted-controller-supplied, verified/content-addressed asset bytes over `stcli-probe://`. The unchanged external HTTPS emblem is consequently a known asset-policy limitation, not a failed proxy requirement. Production integration remains a separate decision.
